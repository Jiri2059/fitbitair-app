Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

FitBit Air Companion (“the App”) is a personal health application that connects to Google Health to display fitness and health data from your Google Fitbit Air device.

1. Data We Access

With your explicit permission, the App requests access to the following data from your Google account via the Google Health API:

  • Activity and fitness data (steps, distance, calories, active minutes, exercises)
  • Health metrics and measurements (heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, SpO₂, skin temperature)
  • Sleep data (duration, stages, efficiency)

We only request read-only access. The App never writes or modifies any of your health data.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data is used solely to display your personal health metrics inside the App (recovery scores, strain, sleep analysis, trends, etc.). We do not use the data for advertising, analytics beyond what is necessary for the App to function, or any other purpose.

3. Data Storage

Health data is processed and temporarily cached on your device to make the App fast and responsive. We do not store your health data on our own servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your health data with any third parties.

5. Your Control

You can revoke the App’s access to your Google Health data at any time in your Google Account settings under “Security” → “Third-party apps with account access”.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: your-email@gmail.com
